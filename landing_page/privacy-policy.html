Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SpellIt. NureHub Studios ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, play matches, or contact support:

  • Account Data: Email address, display name, and profile information via Google/Firebase Authentication.
  • Game Data: Your match history, high scores, ELO rating, virtual currency balance, and unlocked power-ups.
  • Technical Data: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for analytics and bug reporting.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to provide and improve the SpellIt experience, including:

  • Real-time matchmaking and leaderboard rankings.
  • Syncing your game progress across multiple devices.
  • Sending critical game notifications and reminders.
  • Analyzing usage patterns to optimize gameplay and balance.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information as long as your account is active. You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time via the in-app "Delete Account" button or by contacting us at support@nurehub.com.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for authentication, database hosting, and analytics. These services may collect information used to identify you. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@nurehub.com.
